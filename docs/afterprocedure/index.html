Your procedure

After your procedure

Arrange for someone to collect you by car ( you are not insured to drive until the day after your procedure )

You are strongly advised to....

  • Rest on the sofa all evening
  • Put your feet up
  • Paracetemol only as pain killer (if required ) do NOT take either Ibuprofen or Anadin or other NSAID
  • Drink NO ALCOHOL
  • Come to the clinic for all re-dressing appointments

Redressing

When we ask you to redress your toe/s we recommend you follow these steps

  1. take the dressing pack
  2. clean your toe with Clinisept solution (we will supply)
  3. use it
  4. Repeat until xx days or you next viit our clinic

Sports advice

We recommend not playing any sports until we sign you off at your final appointment in our clinic.

This is likely to be 3 - 4 weeks after your little procedure

Guarantee

In the unlikely event that your prcodure is not successful - we will repeat the procedure at a time convenient to you - 100% free of charge.

This has only occurred 2 times in the past 10 years (and once was due to a patient drinking too much alcohol on the evening after their procedure - not recommended !)

All our podiatrists are qualified in Podiatry, are HCPC registered and are members of the Royal College of Podiatry.

We have strong working relationships with local NHS and private Podiatric Surgeons, should a surgical opinion be required, as well as potential onward referrals for imaging, blood tests and therapeutic injections.
